# The artists case study

**The Economics of Superstars: Music Sales and Concerts** is textbook Problem 10.4, the Taylor S. case about an artist's music revenue and concert pricing.

- [Student handout](Artists_Music_and_Concerts.pdf)
- [Editable LyX handout](Artists_Music_and_Concerts.lyx)
- [Canonical questions](../PROBLEM_BANK.md)

Part I can be assigned after CES demand in Chapter 1. The full case additionally uses cost minimization and monopoly pricing. It is placed with the Chapter 10 applied problems and explicitly distinguishes monopoly pricing from the chapter's competitive benchmark.

This is the graduate version of the music-and-concerts case. It specifies CES preferences and decreasing-returns technologies, distinguishes fan wealth from the wage, and separates an artist's share of music volume from a pure royalty fraction. All numerical assumptions are hypothetical. The public handout contains questions only; the current instructor supplement is stored in the private course archive.

The LyX handout is generated from the bank with `python3 scripts/build_artists_case.py`; `--check` verifies synchronization. Compile it with LyX and LaTeX. The main book remains an editable native LyX manuscript.
