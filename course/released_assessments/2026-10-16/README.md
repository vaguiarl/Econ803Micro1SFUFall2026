# Midterm 1: worked solutions

ECON 803, Fall 2026. Exam: October 16. Solutions released October 18, 2026.

- [Download the worked solutions (PDF)](ECON803_Midterm1_Fall2026_Worked_Solutions.pdf)
- [Editable LyX source](ECON803_Midterm1_Fall2026_Worked_Solutions.lyx)

The eight-page student edition covers all ten exam subparts: household search, KKT and corners, AI-agent pricing and welfare, maximum-based revealed preference, and population-WARP welfare bounds. It also includes two optional shorter optimization routes. The LyX file is the editable source for the PDF. No Lean verification is claimed.

## Use the solution actively

1. Read your personalized PDF feedback on Canvas, then attempt the repair before opening the corresponding worked solution.
2. Reproduce one complete household regime: setup, derivatives, active set, feasibility/entry check, budget, utility, and a global-optimality explanation.
3. Complete the two-regime welfare calculation: count, share, correctly directed interval, and economic interpretation. Do not pair observations from separate cross-sections.
4. Close the key and change one parameter or data row. Bring this attempt and your feedback PDF to office hours, marking the first step you cannot justify.

These are minimum practice milestones, not a core-exam passing threshold or a replacement for the full syllabus. A clear plan helps, but practise carrying it through to a verified conclusion.
