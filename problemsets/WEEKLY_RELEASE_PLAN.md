# Fall 2026 weekly problem-set plan

This is the question-only weekly sequence for ECON 803. The textbook's end-of-chapter problems remain the source of record; each packet is a dated view of stable problem identifiers. Chapter case studies are deliberately excluded from this sequence and may be assigned separately. Solutions remain in the private instructor repository.

The **required** column is the board-scale work for class and independent practice. The **extension** column contains a proof or a formal/computational bridge. Order theory is used when needed but remains in the mathematical appendix rather than the main chapter sequence.
